# Synthetic Data Notice

The participant dataset is synthetic and was created for this challenge. It
does not contain Department customer records, real residential street
addresses, or participant-facing internal PII.

Street/address-line fields use the reserved `SYNLOC-` representation. These
values are synthetic location identifiers rather than deliverable postal
addresses.

The challenge evaluation assumes the synthetic identities and location
identifiers remain synthetic. Real-person identification or real-residential
address enrichment is outside the challenge data-use scope.

Higher-level geographic context may reflect public-data calibration used to
make the synthetic dataset realistic.

## Synthetic identity representation

Participant-facing name, date-of-birth, and street/address-line values use
reserved synthetic representations. These values are challenge-only synthetic
data and are not Department customer identities or real residential addresses.
