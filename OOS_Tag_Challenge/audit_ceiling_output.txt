PROBE 1  Can a model that is allowed to memorise reach 90%?
         (training accuracy, no held-out set, deliberately overfitting)
           decision tree, unlimited depth     100.0%
           random forest, unlimited depth     100.0%
           1-nearest-neighbour on itself      100.0%
         -> the features CAN separate the 300 rows when memorised, so the limit
            is not expressive power. It is whether that pattern generalises.

PROBE 2  Do near-identical cases carry the same label?
           label agrees with its 1 nearest twin(s):  49.0%
           label agrees with its 3 nearest twin(s):  48.6%
           label agrees with its 5 nearest twin(s):  47.2%
         -> cases the model literally cannot tell apart disagree ~45% of the time.
            That disagreement is a hard floor on achievable accuracy.

PROBE 3  Does the ground truth agree with itself across phases?
           same verdict at T0 and T1 for the same candidate:  77.7%
           i.e. the official answer changes for 22.3% of candidates after
           just two extra records arrive.

         An oracle that knew the T0 answer perfectly and had to predict T1
         would therefore score 77.7%. That is the practical ceiling for
         'the same case, slightly different evidence'.
